# Questions for a native speaker 🗣️

These words and phrases are in the app with a **⚑ being checked** flag. Free online sources disagreed on them, didn't cover them, or I put them together myself.

**How to use this file:** go through it with your parent and fill in the **Parent says** column:
- ✅ if it's correct as written
- or write the correct / more natural Igbo
- add anything about how *your family* says it

The daily update routine reads this file each morning. Anything answered here gets applied to the app, the ⚑ flag is removed, and the item moves to the **Answered** section at the bottom.

> The app teaches **Standard Igbo** (Igbo Izugbe, based on the Owerri/Umuahia dialects). If your family speaks another dialect (Onitsha, Nsukka, Ngwa, Mbaise…), that's great. Say so below and the app can show your family's version alongside the standard one.

The notes below came from a quick online check by a research helper, and some of it may be wrong, so please judge. Nothing in the app has been changed for these.
