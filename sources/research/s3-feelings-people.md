## 2. Mini-dialogue (uses the items above)

1. **A:** Kedụ ka ị mere? (How are you?) [app greet]
2. **B:** Obi ụtọ na-eme m. Ọrụ ahụ amasịghị m, mana ike gwụrụ m. (I am happy. I did not like that job, but I am tired.) [wv; nk; wv]
3. **A:** Heì! Ị nwaka! (Oh! Well done!) [nk; conv]
4. **B:** Daalụ. Enyi m zụtaara m akpa ojii. (Thanks. My friend bought a black bag for me.) [app; nk example]
5. **A:** Eziokwu? (Really?) [wv; spelling unconfirmed on Nkọwa]
6. **B:** Eziokwu. Ka anyị gaa. (Really. Let's go.) [wv; ig]
7. **A:** Ka ọ dị echi. (See you tomorrow.) [conv]

## 3. Culture notes

- Igbo often describes feelings as things that happen to you: "Iwe na-ewe m" (anger is doing me), "Ụjọ na-atụ m" (fear is holding me). The app's food lesson already uses this pattern.
- "Ndewo" is the traditional hello for any time of day (wv, igboguide). "Daalụ" is the most common thanks, and "Ị meela" is also used for thanks, especially after a meal (wv).
- Greetings to older people are expected. Using a gender word (nwoke, nwaanyị) for someone younger can sound patronising (wv).
- Sympathy: "Ndo" is used to console someone after a misfortune, not usually to apologise (wv). Use "Gbaghara m" for an apology.
- Surprise words are common, and "Chineke" ("God!") is used like "Jesus!" in English (wv).

## 4. Doubts for the parent

1. **Obi ụtọ** (happy): Nkọwa does not have it as a headword. Is "aṅụ̀rị" or "obi ụtọ" the everyday word for happy?
2. **Obi ọjọọ** (sad): The spelling check was not done correctly. Is "obi ọjọọ" right for "unhappy/sad"? Also, the Nkọwa headword for sad is mwute.
3. **Ndo** (sorry): Nkọwa's headword is "ǹdò" (shade). Wikivoyage says it is consolation, not apology. Should the app keep "Ndo: Sorry (sympathy)"?
4. **Ị meela**: The app's `greet-thanks` says "Thank you". Wikivoyage and conv agree on thanks, but the brief says "Well done". Which is right? Should "well done" be "Ị nwaka!" only?
5. **Nnọọ nụ!**: conv says congratulations, but the app uses "nnọọ" for welcome. Which is right?
6. **Chei / Chai / heì**: Nkọwa has heì! (oh!). Which spelling should the app use: Chei, Chai or Heì?
7. **Haa! and Ewo!**: Only seen in an academic paper snippet (not opened). Are these real everyday interjections?
8. **Eziokwu / Ezi okwu**: Nkọwa did not find "eziokwu" as searched. Please confirm the spelling, and whether "Ezi okwu?" (Really?) is a question form.
9. **Obi gị na-eme m** (I miss you): Is this how Igbo speakers say it, or is it a literal translation?
10. **Atụla egwu** (don't be afraid), **Enyi m bụ onye obi ọma** (my friend is kind), **Ọrụ ahụ dị mma?** (how was work?): constructed from patterns, not from a source. Not safe to teach yet.
11. **Bored, excited, clever, fat, slim, quiet, I can't believe it**: no reliable Igbo source found.
12. **Onye agbata obi** (neighbour): Brief form, unconfirmed. Wikivoyage has "àgbàtòobì". Which is standard?
13. **Dialect**: The sources do not say which dialect is used for each phrase, except one wv greeting "northern part of Igboland". Which dialect should the app teach?
