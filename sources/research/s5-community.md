## 3. Culture notes

- Community meetings (nzukọ) and the village assembly were traditionally for adult men, and age grades (ọgbọ) took on shared work, policing and building projects, with members giving both money and labour (WS-A, Ozikoro). Practice has changed, so say "traditionally".
- Kindred (umunna) and age-mate groups are the basis of mutual help. Helping relatives and peers is normal, and a learner is expected to join in when asked.
- Town unions in cities such as Lagos, London and New York often organise development projects and social events, and some are built on age grades (WS-A). The word "town union" has no confirmed Igbo equivalent here.
- Names and roles differ by community: age grades are called ogbo, otu ogbo, ebiri or uke in different places (WS-A). Ask a local speaker before naming a group in a specific town.

## 4. Doubts for the parent

1. **Rubbish:** NK does not give "rubbish". The brief's "ahịhịa" means grass, leaf or weed. The mbuze example uses it for refuse ("Ndị mmadụ na-ekpofu ahịhịa na mbuze ahụ"), but that is one example. What is the usual word for rubbish or refuse (ihe mkpofu, unyi)?
2. **Erosion:** The brief's "iyi ọdịda" was not found in any source (WS-A search gave nothing). NK gives mbuze (erosion/gully). Is mbuze the everyday word? Is "iyi ọdịda" used at all?
3. **Flood:** The word "idei mmiri" was not checked (NK did not load). Which word do speakers use for flood?
4. **Help:** Is "inyere ibe anyị aka" (S4, your sentence) correct? Is "inye aka" (S3) the more common form?
5. **Age grade:** Is ọgbọ used for age grade in your area, or only for "peer"? Is ogbo used instead?
6. **Clean:** Is ọcha the everyday word for clean (not white)? Or should "clean" use another word?
7. **Light:** Is "Ọ dịghị ọkụ" (There is no light) the usual way to say it? Does your area say NEPA or "ọkụ adịghị"?
8. **Umunna:** Confirm the meaning and spelling of umunna (NK did not load).
9. **Tone:** Confirm the tone of mbuze (erosion, mbùzé) and mbụze (landslide, mbụ̀ze). Dictionaries give both.
10. **Constructed sentences:** S10 "Ụzọ a dị njọ", S11 "Obodo anyị dị ọcha" and S15 "Ọ dịghị ọkụ n'obodo anyị" are built from checked parts. Please confirm before use.
